# Sound Squad

Reading practice for kids with dyslexia, set up like **cheer practice**: short routines with an 8-count, spelling cheers ("Give me an R! Give me an AI! Give me an N! What's that spell? RAIN!"), stunt breaks to get moving, and a Coach's Clipboard where a grown-up tracks progress.

It runs in a web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. All progress stays on the device it was entered on. Nothing is uploaded.

## Open it
- **Online:** open the site link in Chrome, Edge or Safari. On a tablet, use *Share → Add to Home Screen* so it opens like an app and works offline.
- **On a Windows computer, from this folder:** double-click `Start Sound Squad.bat` (needs Python installed), then go to `http://localhost:5180`.

## The six activities
| Activity | Skill it practices |
|---|---|
| ⚡ **Speed Warm-Up** | Rapid naming of colors, numbers, letters and quick words (like the CTOPP-2 rapid naming tasks). |
| 🪞 **Mirror Twins** | Telling look-alikes apart: b/d, p/q, u/y, 6/9, 12/21, was/saw. Each pair comes with a "cheer move" to remember it by. |
| 🤸 **Sound Stunts** | Phonological awareness: first, middle and last sounds, counting sounds, taking a sound away (like CTOPP-2 Elision and Phoneme Isolation). |
| 📣 **Give Me a Sound!** | Spelling by sound with tiles, followed by a spelling cheer. Missed words come back as **Comeback words** until they're spelled right twice. |
| 🔁 **Echo Routine** | Working memory: repeat a sequence forward and backward, chunked like counts in a cheer routine. |
| 📖 **Read & Cheer** | Fluency and comprehension: stories in phrase "scoops," then Listen, Echo, and timed *Read it myself* (words correct per minute), then questions on details, main idea, and cause and effect. |

A daily routine is about 10 minutes, with movement breaks. Levels go up after two strong sessions in a row and step back after a rough one. There are no red X's: missed answers get a gentle "rep it again."

## Coach's Clipboard (grown-ups)
Press and hold **Grown-ups** on the home screen. There you can:
- See progress charts, streaks, minutes practiced and the most common mix-ups.
- Change levels and set the font (Lexend, Verdana, Comic Sans), size, spacing, background tint, voice and speed.
- **Record letter sounds in your own voice.** Computer voices can't say a clean /b/, so the app uses your recordings instead.
- Add your own stories, print a progress report, manage several readers, and save or restore backups.
- Turn on an optional microphone reading check. It uses the browser's speech service, which may send audio to Google or Apple. It is off by default.

These are practice measures, not standardized test scores.
