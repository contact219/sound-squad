# Sound Squad

Reading practice for kids with dyslexia, set up like **cheer practice**: short routines with an 8-count, spelling cheers ("Give me an R! Give me an AI! Give me an N! What's that spell? RAIN!"), stunt breaks to get moving, and a Coach's Clipboard where a grown-up tracks progress.

It runs in a web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. All progress, recordings and photos stay on the device. Nothing is uploaded.

## Open it
- **Online:** open the site link in Chrome, Edge or Safari. On a tablet, use *Share → Add to Home Screen* so it opens like an app and works offline.
- **On a Windows computer, from this folder:** double-click `Start Sound Squad.bat` (needs Python installed), then go to `http://localhost:5180`.

## The activities
| Activity | Skill it practices |
|---|---|
| ⚡ **Speed Warm-Up** | Rapid naming of colors, numbers, letters and quick words (like the CTOPP-2 rapid naming tasks). |
| 🪞 **Mirror Twins** | Telling look-alikes apart: b/d, p/q, u/y, 6/9, 12/21, was/saw, each with a "cheer move." |
| 🤸 **Sound Stunts** | Phonological awareness: first, middle and last sounds, counting sounds, taking a sound away. |
| 📣 **Give Me a Sound!** | Spelling with sound tiles, then a spelling cheer. Missed words come back as **Comeback words**. It can use a grown-up's weekly word list. |
| 🏷️ **Cheer Names** | Sounding out made-up words (so memory can't help) and clapping the syllables in long words. |
| 🔁 **Echo Routine** | Working memory: repeat a sequence forward and backward, chunked like counts in a routine. |
| 📖 **Read & Cheer** | Stories in phrase "scoops": Listen, Echo, timed *Read it myself* (words correct per minute, with optional recording), then questions. |
| ✏️ **Sentence Builder** | Say a sentence (speech-to-text or typed by a grown-up), rebuild it from word tiles, fix capitals and end marks, copy it on paper. |
| 🔢 **Math Squad** | Word problems read aloud with visual steps, carrying and borrowing on a place-value chart, telling time, coins. |
| 📷 **Read Anything** | Photograph a worksheet or book page; the words are found on the device and read aloud phrase by phrase. |

A daily routine is about 10 minutes on a three-day rotation, with movement breaks and a "how did it feel?" check at the end. Levels adjust on their own. There are no red X's.

## Coach's Clipboard (grown-ups)
Press and hold **Grown-ups** on the home screen.
- **Progress:** charts for every activity, focus and attention (minutes, activities stopped early, best time of day), how practice felt, trouble spots, notes, and a **weekly summary** you can print or share.
- **Checkpoints:** a 10-minute monthly check using the same lists every time (letter sounds, 1-minute real words, 1-minute made-up words, 10 spelling words, recorded story reading), with month-to-month charts.
- **Sound map:** every letter sound colored by accuracy. Star the sounds to focus on, and they appear more often in practice.
- **Goals:** set targets (e.g. "60 words correct per minute by May") and watch the progress bars.
- **Recordings & writing:** play reading recordings and compare the first with the latest; see the sentence journal.
- **Voice & video:** record letter sounds in your own voice, short mouth videos, and cheer-move videos.
- **Words & stories:** this week's spelling words (split into sound tiles automatically) and your own stories.
- **Settings, readers and backup.**

Speech-to-text is optional and off by default. It uses the browser's speech service, which may send audio to Google or Apple. These are practice measures, not standardized test scores.
